The White Silence: Free 3D and VR Winter Survival Game

The White Silence is a free open-world winter survival game that runs in your web browser on phones, tablets and computers, with WebXR VR support for headsets such as Meta Quest.

Features

  • Huge frozen open world with abandoned houses, a gas station, mining town, research station, military depot, frozen lake and highway
  • Survival stats: health, hunger, thirst and body warmth, with blizzards and a day and night cycle
  • Hunt deer, rabbits and wolves, build campfires, and construct a shed log by log
  • Drive a truck or camper, fly a plane or helicopter
  • Meet survivors, trade supplies and explore with a minimap
  • VR controls: swing to chop, rub hands to light a fire, grab the steering wheel

THE WHITE SILENCE

The plane is down. Civilization is gone. Only winter remains.

Move: left thumb. Look: drag right side. ACT: chop trees, hunt, grab fuel cans, refuel truck. FIRE: 2 wood. EAT/DRINK: near a fire to cook and melt snow. VEHICLE: enter/exit the truck (up/down = drive, left/right = steer). In a car, keep the fire lit and the heater on.
